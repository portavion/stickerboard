stickerboard privacy notice

Effective 2026-09-09

The short version

  • stickerboard has no account, no servers, and no analytics. It asks for no Android permissions at all.
  • Your stickers stay in stickerboard's private storage on your phone.
  • Sending a sticker hands that one image to the chat app you chose, and nowhere else.
  • Uninstalling deletes every sticker.

Where your stickers live

Your stickers live in a folder only stickerboard can open.

Android backup is off, so stickers are not copied into a cloud backup.

Adding stickers

You add stickers with the photo picker, by sharing an image to stickerboard, or by pasting.

Each path copies the image you picked into stickerboard's own folder. The original is untouched.

stickerboard does not browse your gallery. The system picker hands over only what you selected. That is why there is no photo permission.

Sending a sticker

Tapping a sticker gives the chat app temporary read access to that one image file, through Android's file-sharing mechanism.

stickerboard does not upload it anywhere. What the chat app does with it afterwards is that app's business and its own privacy policy.

If the chat app cannot take a sticker directly, stickerboard falls back to the clipboard.

A clipboard entry can be read by whichever app you paste into next, and on some Android versions by the system clipboard UI.

That is one sticker at a time, and only when you tapped send.

Backing up to a zip

Export writes a zip to the location you chose in the system file picker. That can be Downloads, Drive, a USB stick, or whatever you picked.

Once it is there, stickerboard has no say over it. Its privacy is your file manager's or your cloud provider's.

Import reads a zip you choose. stickerboard never writes to shared storage on its own.

The cutout tool

Cutouts run on your device, with Google's ML Kit subject segmentation.

The first cutout may make Google Play services download the segmentation model to your phone.

That download follows Google's privacy policy. It carries no sticker of yours.

Your image is never sent anywhere for this. The pixels go from stickerboard's folder to on-device code and back.

Deleting your stickers

Delete one with the × on its thumbnail.

Delete all of them by uninstalling stickerboard. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children

stickerboard is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, and it has no age-gated surface.

Changes to this notice

If a future version of stickerboard collects anything, this page changes in the same release that adds it, and the effective date at the top moves.
